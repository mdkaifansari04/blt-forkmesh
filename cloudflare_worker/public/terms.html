ForkMesh Terms

Last updated June 20, 2026.

Short version: ForkMesh is experimental distributed code-hosting software. Use it for public repositories you have the right to mirror, keep your keys safe, and do not send BCH to an expired join address.

Using ForkMesh

You are responsible for the repositories, messages, issues, pull requests, profile data, and payment addresses you publish through ForkMesh. Do not use ForkMesh to host or distribute content you do not have permission to share.

Joining the network

The join flow may ask you to donate Bitcoin Cash (BCH) to a one-time address. That address expires after one hour if it receives no transactions. When it expires, ForkMesh hides it, warns you not to send funds, and removes the unpaid address from the system after five more minutes. Generate a fresh address instead.

Rewards and availability

Any payout, reward, uptime, or earnings display is experimental and may change. Running a node does not guarantee payment, availability, or data recovery. You are responsible for backing up your own repositories and keys.

Third parties

We try our best not to use third parties. The current public service still involves a few:

  • Cloudflare for hosting, Workers, D1 storage, caching, and relay infrastructure.
  • Blockchair for Bitcoin Cash address lookups and transaction broadcast support.
  • Bundled open-source font files served locally by ForkMesh; no remote font service is used.
  • Tailwind Play CDN on the current home page.
  • The Bitcoin Cash network for payment settlement.
  • Any Git hosting provider or remote repository you choose to mirror.

No warranty

ForkMesh is provided as-is. The software and network may have bugs, outages, incomplete features, or data loss. Use it at your own risk.
