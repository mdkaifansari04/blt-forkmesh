Non-custodial payout wallet Your payout address belongs to you. Wallet keys and recovery phrases stay on your device; ForkMesh never asks you to upload them. User-owned funds, community-pool funds, pending rewards, and completed on-chain transfers are separate states. The public community pool is signed only by the first instance owner’s local Qt client; the Worker stores unsigned plans and finalized public signatures, never the pool private key. Rewards are community incentives, not investments or guaranteed returns.
