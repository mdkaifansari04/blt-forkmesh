Privacy

ForkMesh Privacy

Last updated June 20, 2026.

Short version: We try our best not to use third parties or collect extra data. ForkMesh still needs some account, node, repository, and payment data to run the network.

What is public

Your node name, public key, advertised repositories, public profile details, issue and pull-request records, online presence, and any payout Solana address you publish may be visible to other users or visitors.

What is stored

ForkMesh stores account records, encrypted account details, repository catalog data, relay presence data, pending verification records, and operational logs needed to keep the service working. Join payment addresses are temporary: unpaid addresses expire after one hour, are hidden with a warning, and are removed after a five-minute grace period.

Payments

Solana transactions are public on the Solana network. ForkMesh checks whether a join payment reference has received enough SOL to activate the next signup step. Do not send funds once the signup page says the payment request is expiring.

Third parties

We try our best not to use third parties. Today, these are involved:

  • Cloudflare hosts the public website, Worker APIs, D1 data, cache, and relay infrastructure.
  • A Solana JSON-RPC endpoint is queried for payment-reference status.
  • Bundled open-source font files are served locally by ForkMesh; no remote font service is used.
  • Tailwind Play CDN is loaded by the current home page.
  • The Solana network processes payment transactions.
  • Git remotes you choose may receive network requests when you mirror or sync repositories.

Your choices

You can avoid adding optional profile fields, avoid publishing a payout address, stop running the desktop node, or use your own repository mirrors. Public blockchain transactions cannot be made private by ForkMesh after they are sent.
