Careers

Help us put ForkMesh on every developer's machine.

We've built code hosting that can't be taken away. Now we need someone to make sure the developers who need it actually hear about it.

Why join us

ForkMesh is a small, founder-led team building a resilient, peer-to-peer alternative to centralized code forges. The product works, the network is live, and the story — code that can't be deleted out from under you — resonates the moment people hear it. What we're missing is reach. That's where you come in.

Open roles

Marketing Lead — Launch & Growth Remote · Full-time or contract
Developer marketing Growth Community Early-stage

We're looking for a marketer who can take ForkMesh from a great product to mass adoption. You'll own how we reach developers, tell our story, and turn first-time visitors into nodes that stay on the network. This is a build-it-from-scratch role with real ownership and a direct line to the founders.

What you'll do

  • Own and execute the launch plan to drive mass adoption — from Hacker News, Product Hunt and Reddit to dev newsletters, YouTube and conferences.
  • Build a repeatable growth engine: positioning, messaging, landing pages, and the funnel from visit to signup to active node.
  • Grow and energize a developer community across X/Twitter, Discord, and open-source channels.
  • Create content that developers actually want — technical posts, demos, comparisons, and the founding story of code that can't be erased.
  • Set up analytics and report on what's working, doubling down on the channels that compound.

What we're looking for

  • A track record of growing a developer-focused or technical product, ideally open source or dev-tools.
  • Strong writing and an instinct for what makes engineers click, share, and sign up.
  • Comfort owning a launch end-to-end with little hand-holding — scrappy, fast, and data-aware.
  • Genuine excitement for decentralization, open source, and giving developers control of their own work.

How to apply: email us at founders@forkmesh.com with a short note on how you'd launch ForkMesh, plus anything you've grown before (links welcome). No formal cover letter needed — show us how you think.

Don't see your role?

We're early and growing. If you believe in code that can't be taken away and think you can help us get there, tell us how — email the founders at founders@forkmesh.com.
