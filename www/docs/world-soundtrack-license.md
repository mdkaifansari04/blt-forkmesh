# ForkMesh World Song

The World entrance sign plays *ForkMesh Forever (Indie Pop)*, a first-party
track published by the project at
`world/public/assets/songs/ForkMeshForever(IndiePop).mp3`. It is
served from ForkMesh’s own origin, starts only after that sign is clicked,
plays once on the visitor’s device, and is never streamed from or relayed to a
third-party provider. The World sound control stops it immediately.

All other World sounds are short event effects synthesized locally with the
Web Audio API. No ambient music catalog or procedural soundtrack is bundled.
